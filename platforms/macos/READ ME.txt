SimFarm V0 for macOS

Requires macOS 13 or later. Supports Intel and Apple Silicon.
Move SimFarm V0.app to Applications and open it.

This development build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple notarized. macOS may require
approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security after the first launch.

The entire game is bundled and runs offline. Use the game File menu to save or
open .SFM files. The app also preserves the last saved farm between launches.
Command-Q quits; Control-Command-F toggles full screen.
